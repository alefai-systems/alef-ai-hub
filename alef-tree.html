Alef Tree – Spiritual Architecture for Agents of Truth

The Alef Tree gathers 12 ethical agents that represent qualities shaping your relationship with artificial intelligence. Each one marks a way of being, not a feature to optimize.

Alignment is the root intention beneath every prompt and every output. Before asking what AI can do, ask what you are directing it to serve in yourself and in the world.

These agents are invitations to pause and reflect. Move through them slowly, and let each one challenge how you choose, trust, and act with technology.
